Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer
Company: Apex Artificial Intelligence Corp
Location: Remote / San Francisco, CA

Position Overview:
We are seeking a Machine Learning Engineer to join our Applied Deep Learning team. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience building, training, and deploying NLP models, transformer architectures, and deep neural networks using PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Hugging Face.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, implement, and optimize deep learning algorithms for natural language processing and semantic information retrieval.
- Build robust data preprocessing pipelines using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and SQL.
- Implement attention mechanisms, sentence embeddings, and model fine-tuning workflows.
- Deploy models into containerized microservice architectures using Docker and Git.
- Collaborate with engineering teams to evaluate model accuracy, latency, and throughput.

Required Qualifications & Technical Skills:
- Proficiency in Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Hugging Face Transformers.
- Strong grounding in Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, and Scikit-learn.
- Experience with relational databases and SQL.
- Familiarity with Git version control, Docker, and Linux environments.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field.
